<objective>
Add the packCount field (number of boxes/packs) to the stock data model and all form touchpoints. This is the schema half of the G-05-2 gap: users need to record how many boxes of a medicine they have, separate from how many units (tablets) are in each box.

Purpose: Without packCount, Open box cannot know how many packs exist, and users have no way to express "2 boxes of 30 tablets" vs "60 loose tablets".
Output: db.version(5) migration, updated Medicine interface, updated StockFields form and schema, and updated callers (StockEditSheet, new.tsx).
</objective>
