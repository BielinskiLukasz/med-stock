### Phase 05 Override (SC4)

ROADMAP SC4 specified "status derived from the nearest-expiry active stock entry" but the implementation uses `computeCatalogAggregate` priority-reduce (worst-case status across all active stocks). Gap closure G-05-4 (plan 05-08) intentionally replaced the nearest-expiry approach to fix ExceededOpenPeriod entries that have no expiry date. The deviation is strictly correct and superior. Override accepted by `lukasz.bielinski` on 2026-08-29.
